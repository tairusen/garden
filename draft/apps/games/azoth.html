THE CRAFT

  • Swap adjacent sigils. Only swaps that make a match of 3+ will hold.
  • Bane: the creature takes double damage from this sigil.
  • Hunger: match it and the creature attacks at once. Otherwise its wrath builds one pip per move; it strikes at the last pip.
