AI Policy

TL;DR

None of the text in any blog or post that you see has been generated by AI.


However, AI has been used to brainstorm and generate code that is in current use. It is also used to generate documentation that I do not have the time to create.

The Longer Part

Finding Balance

Operating under the assumption that AI is useless or weak is foolish. However, on the other side of this digital coin, is operating under the assumption that AI is superior. Which is also, foolish. Having a balanced view of AI, that it can make you a faster (not better!) creator, will allow you to use it to it's maximum potential, without overusing it or underusing it.

Acheiving this balance is impossible, and I do not. I err on the side of caution, and on the side of desiring for human expression within content that I create.

Which is why I will not use AI to write any of my blogs, posts, or anywhere that I want another human being to read or care. AI can generate code and documentation that doesn't need to have the level of expressionism that I desire. That is the extent of it's usefulness to me.

Utilizing AI to Program

I personally have deep knowledge of computer systems, both software and hardware. I have earned this knowledge through constant self-study, and it has granted me the ability to understand most of anything I use AI to generate. Therefor, to my use case, using AI to generate code is a timesaver, and is merely keeping from me the trouble of disecting and creating monotonous things that just slow one down.

For instance, this site was generated by GLM-5.3-Flash. I had a plan in mind for what I wanted a website to look like, and over the course of a few days worked with the model to create this site. And I personally love the outcome, I find that my specification and stylization was met to the standards that I found acceptable. You can read more on how I made the site once the blog gets released.

I do not, however, use AI to do things that I cannot understand. If I need to learn something in order to accomplish it, when AI could do said thing for me, I will pause, learn how to do it myself, learn the concepts behind it, and then proceed, either by myself, or with AI.

The point of creation is the fun of it. Having AI make everything for me while I just sit back and scroll on my phone both teaches me nothing and results in zero joy. Asking something to do something for you, then seeing the finished product, does not spark the same satisfaction then doing it yourself. AI cannot take what I do away from me, because it is me and my brain that is needed to do whatever I am doing, because it is for my pleasure, and not for the sole sake of creating a finished prodct. It's like working for profit verus working for fun. The fun is the process, not the finished product.
